Block is two things in one app: Bitcoin at a glance on your home screen, and a Nostr social client for the people who talk about it.

<b>Bitcoin widgets</b>
<ul>
<li>Home-screen widgets in a desk-blockclock style: price, sats per dollar or baht, block height, fees, hash rate, difficulty, halving countdown, Lightning capacity, Strategy (MSTR) and Strive (ASST) holdings, and the value of your stack.</li>
<li>Price chart widget (24H / 7D / 30D / 1Y), halving countdown and Bitcoin quotes. Every widget is resizable.</li>
<li>USD or THB, with your choice of price source.</li>
</ul>

<b>Nostr social</b>
<ul>
<li>A timeline laid out like X: notes, replies, reposts, quotes, likes and zaps.</li>
<li>Private messages and group chats (NIP-17), lists, bookmarks, live streams and long-form articles.</li>
<li>Photo albums, videos, polls, link previews, and live cards for Bitcoin transactions and blocks in posts.</li>
<li>A Lightning wallet tab with Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC): balance, receive with a QR code, send by pasting or scanning an invoice, and zaps in one tap.</li>
<li>Your relays your way: outbox model, relay checkup and suggestions, NIP-11 info.</li>
<li>Themes including Ember from Zeus.</li>
<li>English and Thai.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
No account of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Market data comes from public sources such as mempool.space; social data from the Nostr relays you choose. Your key stays on your phone (encrypted with the Android Keystore) or in your signer app (Amber, or a remote signer).

Block shows information only. It is not an exchange and does not give financial advice.
